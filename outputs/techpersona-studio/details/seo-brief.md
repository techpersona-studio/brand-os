### 1. Primary SEO goal

TechPersona Studio needs to be found by service business owners in the Worcester, MA area (primary) and nationally (secondary) who are searching for someone to redesign their website. The website redesign service is the SEO entry point — confirmed strategy for launch: easier to convert, easier to collect early reviews. Automation is a long-term upsell and secondary SEO target. At launch, a single-page site is the right call: the domain is new, proof is early-stage, and concentrating SEO signal on one URL outperforms diluting it across several thin pages. The page must be built with clean section structure so individual sections can be promoted to standalone URLs later without restructuring.

---

### 2. Homepage target

**Primary search theme:** Website redesign for service businesses in Worcester, MA — covering queries like "website redesign Worcester MA," "small business website redesign near me," "service business website Worcester," and problem-framed variants like "my website isn't getting me calls."

**Location terms on homepage:** Worcester MA belongs in the homepage H1 or subtitle and in the opening body text. Local SEO is the priority at launch; the local qualifier also signals relevance and authenticity to small business owners who prefer working with someone nearby. "Worcester, MA and beyond" framing keeps the door open for national clients without burying the local signal.

**What it should not try to rank for:** Automation-specific terms on the homepage — those belong in the services section and will eventually have their own URL. Do not try to rank for automation queries and website redesign queries simultaneously from the homepage; neither will rank well if the page sends mixed signals.

### 4. Required content at launch (single-page architecture)

The site launches as a single page. Each section below maps to a scrollable section with a clean ID anchor (e.g., `#services`, `#about`, `#audit`). These IDs should be treated as future URL slugs — if the site expands to multi-page later, `/services` and `/about` can become standalone URLs with no structural refactoring required.

**Hero section — `#hero`**
- SEO purpose: Establishes primary topic and location signal immediately
- Must contain: H1 naming service + location (e.g., "Website redesign for service businesses in Worcester, MA"); problem statement in visible text; primary CTA to the audit section; founder name (Thao Phuong) present in body text or subheading

**Services section — `#services`**
- SEO purpose: Makes both services crawlable and named in page body text
- Must contain: Website redesign described with explicit service language (what is built, who it's for); Quiet Systems described with explicit automation language (what it catches, what it automates); H2 headings for each service; internal anchor link to audit section from each service description

**Proof / case study section — `#work`**
- SEO purpose: Named client (Quán Phở) with before/after content anchors trust signals in crawlable text
- Must contain: Client name "Quán Phở" in crawlable text (confirmed named attribution); before/after comparison with descriptive alt text on every image; honest framing about new site under development ("rebuilt — new site live soon" or equivalent); no fabricated metrics or star counts

**About / founder section — `#about`**
- SEO purpose: Establishes founder identity and credential signals; supports branded searches for "Thao Phuong" and "TechPersona Studio"
- Must contain: Founder name (Thao Phuong) in an H2 or prominent text element; 6-year engineering credential in crawlable body text; founder portrait with descriptive alt text ("Thao Phuong, founder of TechPersona Studio"); Worcester MA location mentioned; direct-access promise in text

**FAQ section — `#faq`**
- SEO purpose: Captures objection-stage queries; eligible for FAQPage schema and FAQ rich results
- Must contain: Structured Q&A format (not flowing prose); minimum 6 questions covering process, pricing model, trust, timeline, and the audit offer; answers in complete sentences

**Audit / contact section — `#audit`**
- SEO purpose: Conversion section; descriptive heading makes the offer crawlable and findable
- Must contain: H2 naming the free audit offer explicitly; what happens after submission (audit within 2 days, discovery call follows); Formspree form embedded on-page (visitor never leaves the site); contact email and phone in crawlable text for NAP; no competing CTAs in this section

### 10. Schema recommendations

**LocalBusiness — top of page (homepage `<head>`)**
- Why it matters: Establishes business identity, Worcester MA address, phone, email, and service category in Google's knowledge graph. Put in place at launch even before GBP is live — GBP will pull from it.
- Required fields: `name`, `address` (Worcester MA), `telephone`, `email`, `url`, `serviceArea`, `foundingDate`
- Add `sameAs` array: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, GBP once each is live (add URLs as profiles are created this week)

**Person — about section schema**
- Why it matters: Thao Phuong is the trust model. Person schema ties her name, role, and domain together for Google's entity graph.
- Required fields: `name`, `jobTitle`, `worksFor`, `url`, `image`

**FAQPage — FAQ section**
- Why it matters: Structured FAQ can trigger FAQ rich results in SERPs, expanding SERP real estate for objection-stage queries without a separate content investment.
- Apply to the FAQ section Q&A block. Each question/answer pair wrapped in `Question` + `Answer` types.

**Do not add yet:** Review / AggregateRating schema. No reviews exist. Empty or fabricated schema risks a manual action from Google. Add after first real reviews are collected (GBP or direct testimonials).

### 11. Technical SEO must-haves

**Single-page architecture at launch:** Appropriate for the launch stage. Use descriptive section IDs (`#services`, `#about`, `#audit`, `#faq`, `#work`) — not generic IDs like `#section-2`. These IDs serve as future URL slugs when the site expands to multi-page.

**URL conventions (for future multi-page):** When pages are split out, use: `/website-redesign`, `/quiet-systems`, `/about`, `/audit`. Lowercase, hyphenated, no query strings.

**Canonical domain:** Confirmed — `www.techpersonastudio.com`. Non-www (`techpersonastudio.com`) already 301-redirects to www. No action needed — this is correctly configured. Keep it as-is.

**Title/meta for single page:** One `<title>` tag. Suggested format: `Website Redesign for Service Businesses in Worcester, MA | TechPersona Studio`. Meta description should name the offer, the audience, the location, and include a soft CTA.

**Heading hierarchy:** Single H1 on the page (hero). Each major section gets an H2. Sub-points within sections use H3. Never skip levels. Never use heading tags for visual styling only.

**Sitemap:** XML sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` referencing the homepage. Submit to Google Search Console once the domain is verified. Verify the domain now — don't wait until launch.

**robots.txt:** Standard allow-all. No pages to block on a single-page site; just ensure the file exists and references the sitemap.

**Image alt text:** Every image requires descriptive alt text.
- Founder portrait: "Thao Phuong, founder of TechPersona Studio, Worcester MA"
- Quán Phở before: describe the old site's key visual state ("Quán Phở restaurant website before redesign — [describe key characteristics]")
- Quán Phở after: describe the new site's key characteristics

**NAP consistency:** Set NAP in the footer now. Use exactly the same name, address, phone, and email when GBP is created this weekend and when social profiles are set up this week. Never abbreviate "TechPersona Studio" — always the full name.

**Form:** Formspree handles submission and email notification. Form stays on the page; visitor never redirects. Ensure the Formspree endpoint is not indexable (it won't be by default, but confirm robots settings on the Formspree side).

**Founder portrait (Core Web Vitals):** WebP format, responsive `srcset`, eager-load (`loading="eager"`) — hero images above the fold must not be lazy-loaded. All below-fold images: lazy-load (`loading="lazy"`).

**Animations:** `transform` and `opacity` only; all transitions guarded by `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` media query. The restrained motion model already supports good CLS and FID scores.
