# Website Blueprint Handoff
**Client:** TechPersona Studio
**Website:** https://www.techpersonastudio.com/
**Date:** June 24, 2026

---

## 1. Current site verdict

- **Overall status:** Functioning — clear offer, low-commitment CTA, solid voice; not yet converting at ceiling due to thin proof layer
- **Core conversion problem:** No external validation for a brand-new solo operator; trust burden is high and the site doesn't currently carry it
- **Core trust problem:** One incomplete case study, no testimonials; self-attribution without third-party signal
- **Core style problem:** Cannot verify visual execution from text extraction; risk is that visual quality lags behind copy quality, creating a tone mismatch
- **Core structural problem:** CTA hierarchy split in hero; "Quiet Systems" is too abstract to self-qualify buyers; no CTA after FAQ; proof is concentrated in one section rather than distributed throughout the page

---

## 2. Recommended intervention level

**Keep and tune**

- **Why:** Core structure, messaging, and offer architecture are sound. The gaps are evidence gaps (proof, outcomes, testimonials), not structural design gaps. A redesign now would be the wrong ROI move — the site needs real proof assets, not new layouts.
- **Scope implication:** No new pages required now. Targeted copy and content fixes. Proof modules added as assets become available. One CTA hierarchy fix in the hero. One scenario added to the Quiet Systems section.

---

## 3. What to keep

- Hero headline — specific, outcome-oriented, names the real fear
- "The Leak" section structure — pain-first framing with a real statistic
- Customer journey steps — frames both services in a single narrative arc
- Free audit CTA architecture — named deliverables, timeline, no-commitment language, personal sign-off
- Process section — low-risk framing with explicit free steps and guarantee
- FAQ content — pre-empts all major objections; strong self-selection filter
- Animated proof block — specific, visual, outcome-oriented; one of the strongest trust moments on the page
- Founder voice throughout — the writing is the differentiator; do not homogenize it
- "Who You're Hiring" section framing — addresses the solo operator objection directly
- Pull quote — right tone, right message; needs a testimonial nearby to land fully
- Form design — lean, low-friction, explicit trust line

---

## 4. What to remove

- Second hero CTA ("See what I build") as a co-equal button — demote to subordinate text link
- CTA label inconsistency ("Get in touch" vs. "Get my free audit") — standardize to one label across all CTA surfaces

---

## 5. What to fix

1. **Case study outcomes:** Add measurable result to Quán Phở case study — bookings, leads, or a named client quote with a specific outcome. Without numbers, it's aesthetic proof, not business proof.
2. **Testimonials:** Add the first 1–2 client testimonials as soon as available. Place one near "Who You're Hiring," one near the audit form. Named, with photo and business context.
3. **Hero CTA hierarchy:** One dominant CTA (audit → terracotta island button). "See what I build" becomes a ghost/text link below it. One action per screen.
4. **Quiet Systems concreteness:** Add one scenario sentence that shows what the system actually does — a specific trigger, action, and outcome (e.g., "A lead texts at 11pm; they get a confirmation and booking link in two minutes, while you're asleep."). Removes abstraction without adding length.
5. **FAQ-to-CTA gap:** Add a short CTA at the base of the FAQ section. High-intent visitors who read through FAQs are one step from acting — give them the step.
6. **Proof distribution:** Move at least one trust signal (proof chip, stat reference, or early outcome mention) closer to the top half of the page. Currently all proof is mid-to-bottom.
7. **Phone number placement:** Surface the phone number near the audit CTA or form section, not only in the nav. Some buyers want to call before they fill out a form.
8. **Pricing expectation management:** Add a soft pricing signal somewhere before the form — not a full price list, but a range or framing ("Most first projects are one-time, not a retainer — typically a few thousand dollars, scoped after the audit"). Reduces avoidance reads on "we scope it together."

## 6. Page / structure implications

**Current pages that are weak:**
- The "Quiet Systems" description block — too abstract to convert visitors unfamiliar with automation.

**Current pages performing adequately:**
- Homepage as a whole — single-page structure is the right call for this stage. No reason to expand before proof is built.

**Pages missing (not critical now, needed at scale):**
- Dedicated case study page for Quán Phở once outcome data is confirmed — gives the case study room to breathe and becomes a linkable proof asset
- A service breakdown page for Quiet Systems once the offer has traction — too early now, but needed when that service generates its own inbound interest
- A lightweight "about" page or extended founder section once there are 3+ testimonials to support it

**Pages to add when proof exists:**
- Testimonials / results page — consolidates social proof as the review count grows; feeds SEO with industry + location keywords

---

## 7. Conversion implications

- **Homepage must:** Get the primary CTA above the fold with one dominant action; get at least one proof signal into the first scroll; close with the audit form at the bottom
- **Trust must appear:** Near the hero (animated proof block), near "Who You're Hiring" (testimonial), and near the form (case study outcome or trust line)
- **CTA must:** Use one consistent label across all surfaces; appear after the Leak section, in the hero, after the FAQ, and at the close; the terracotta island button is reserved for one dominant action per section
- **Proof must:** Include at least one named external source (testimonial with business name and result) before the site can convert cold traffic reliably; the Quán Phở case study needs a measurable outcome
- **Booking flow must:** Remain single-form; keep "no call required" language; add phone number near form for buyers who prefer synchronous contact
- **Mobile must:** Surface the audit CTA within the first screen; ensure the animated proof block renders legibly at small sizes; FAQ should accordion cleanly; form should complete without horizontal scroll

---

## 8. SEO / architecture implications

- **Homepage target issue:** From the copy alone, the site targets "website design for service businesses" thematically. Primary audience is local service businesses (restaurants, realtors, nail salons, plumbers). Location-specific targeting is absent from visible page copy — no city or region anchors, no local service area language. Low priority now, but a gap as local SEO becomes a lead channel.
- **Missing service / FAQ / trust pages:** No standalone service page for "Quiet Systems" — limits discoverability for buyers searching "automated lead follow-up for restaurants" or similar. No standalone case study page — limits the SEO and shareability value of the Quán Phở proof.
- **Internal linking issue:** Single-page architecture has no internal linking to optimize. No issues now; monitor if multi-page structure is added later.
- **Page architecture issue:** If the site expands beyond one page, the blog or resources section should target bottom-of-funnel queries (e.g., "why restaurants lose leads after hours," "how to respond to leads faster") rather than generic educational content.
- **Technical / structural concern:** Cannot verify page speed, structured data, or indexability from text extraction. Recommend confirming: Core Web Vitals passing, proper title/meta description on the homepage, and Google Business Profile linked once set up.

---

## 9. Final blueprint notes

- Site is in a proof-building window, not a redesign window. The right work is gathering and publishing evidence — not restructuring the layout.
- The animated proof block and the Quán Phở case study are the two strongest assets. Both need outcome data to unlock their full value.
- The voice and copy quality are well above standard for a solo operator at this stage. Do not dilute them in future updates.
- Priority build order for proof assets: (1) Quán Phở outcome data + owner quote, (2) first 1–2 testimonials with photos.
- The Quiet Systems section needs one concrete scenario sentence — this is a 15-minute copy fix, not a redesign task.
- When the first 3–5 testimonials are in, revisit whether a partial redesign is warranted to give proof more structural weight on the page.
- No major blockers to conversion except proof availability. Everything else is tunable without a rebuild.
- Known assets available: Quán Phở before/after (visual incomplete), founder portrait (referenced but not confirmed published), animated proof chip (live).
- Known missing: Named testimonials, case study outcome metrics, local SEO anchors.
