## 4. Brand Personality & Voice

**Core traits:**
- Direct — short declarative sentences, no hedging language, no jargon
- Calm — "quiet" used as a product metaphor and recurring theme across hero, services, FAQ
- Founder-present — first-person singular throughout ("I build," "I'm Thao," "I review," "I send back")
- Honest — proactive about what the offer does not include ("nothing owed, nothing to cancel," "yours to keep regardless")
- Technically grounded — 6 years software engineering framed as credibility signal, not decorative biography

— Confirmed (strong) for direct, calm, founder-present, honest. Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ — hero, about, FAQ, services, closing sections (tone audit)
— Confirmed (partial) for technically grounded. Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ — about section

**Anti-traits:**
- Not hype-driven — no superlatives, no "blazing fast," no "world-class" claims
- Not corporate/agency — no "our team," no plural voice, no partner logos or awards section
- Not jargon-heavy — no mention of "conversion rate optimization," "funnel," "growth hacking"
- Not commitment-heavy — pricing model and CTA explicitly remove pressure ("nothing to cancel," "scope before you commit")
- Not broad/unfocused — positioned narrowly for service businesses, not "any business"

— Confirmed (strong). Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ — full homepage tone audit; absence of above patterns confirmed

**Voice rules:**
- Write in first-person singular. Never "we."
- Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence.
- Name the problem before the solution. Lead with the cost of inaction.
- Use "quiet," "runs in the background," "before they call the next name" as recurring vocabulary anchors.
- Avoid: "agency," "solution," "innovative," "seamless," "robust," "leverage," "utilize."
- Offer proof or specifics before stating a claim. Do not lead with adjectives.

— Confirmed (strong). Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ — tone audit of homepage copy

**Messaging patterns:**
- "[Problem that happens while owner is working] → [consequence they don't see] → [Quiet Systems fixes it]"
- "One person. [Direct access promise]. [Trust anchor]."
- "Free audit first — no commitment, yours to keep."
- "Before they call the next name on the list."
- "The site gets found. The system catches what the site doesn't."

— Confirmed (strong). Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ — hero, problem section, services section, closing CTA

**Words to avoid:**
- "Agency" — positions opposite to the solo personal brand
- "Solution/solutions" — avoid in copy entirely
- "Seamless" — overused in web design marketing; undercuts the direct voice
- "Full-service" — implies team/scale the solo model cannot back up
- "Digital" — generic modifier; adds no meaning

— Recommended (strong). Signals: brand voice analysis + confirmed anti-traits. Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ tone audit

**Brand archetype (optional):** The Craftsperson / Maker — solo practitioner who builds with precision and stands behind the work personally.
— Recommended (strong). Not client-confirmed. Signals: "one person on your project," "I build," direct access promise, audit-first model. Source: https://www.techpersonastudio.com/ hero and about section
