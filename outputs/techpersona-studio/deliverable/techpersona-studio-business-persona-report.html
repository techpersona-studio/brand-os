Overview

Executive summary

TechPersona Studio is a solo web and automation studio that opened in June 2026, serving small service business owners who are significantly behind on technology. The studio builds websites that get found and lead-catching systems that run without the owner's attention, sold as one project with one-time pricing and no retainer. Thao Phuong stays on every project from audit to launch.

  • The audience is small service businesses that are notably behind on tech, not growth-stage companies. First targets: restaurants, nail salons, realtors, and plumbers, reached via warm outreach.
  • The website should optimize for one action: a free website audit. The CTA goes to a form. The audit report comes back within 2 days. A discovery call follows to discuss website redesign, automation, or both.
  • The biggest conversion challenge is trust. The studio opened in June 2026 with no external reviews yet. Proof is actively being built: free redesigns in exchange for reviews, 2 case studies in progress, 2-3 reviews expected within 1 month, and Google Business Profile plus social pages launching this week.
  • The brand voice is direct, calm, and founder-present. First-person singular throughout. No hype, no agency framing, no jargon.
  • All client questions are answered and this brief is ready for the website blueprint. Fields marked "Recommended" are inferred from strong signals in site copy and are labeled accordingly.

Fields marked "Recommended" are inferred from strong signals in the site copy and research. They are not yet client-confirmed and are labeled accordingly throughout.

Voice rules
Confirmed
  • Write in first-person singular. Never "we."
  • Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence.
  • Name the problem before the solution. Lead with the cost of inaction.
  • Use "quiet," "runs in the background," "before they call the next name" as recurring vocabulary anchors.
  • Offer proof or specifics before stating a claim. Do not lead with adjectives.
  • Avoid: "agency," "solution," "innovative," "seamless," "leverage," "utilize."
Messaging patterns
Confirmed
  • "[Problem that happens while owner is working] — [consequence they don't see] — [Quiet Systems fixes it]"
  • "One person. [Direct access promise]. [Trust anchor]."
  • "Free audit first — no commitment, yours to keep."
  • "Before they call the next name on the list."
  • "The site gets found. The system catches what the site doesn't."
Words to avoid in copy
Recommended
Agency Solution Seamless Full-service Digital

Keep "solution" out of copy entirely. The studio name and voice are built on precision, not category language.
