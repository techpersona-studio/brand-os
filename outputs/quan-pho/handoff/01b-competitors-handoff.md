## Category patterns

- Common CTAs: Order online, reservations, delivery, menu access
- Common proof signals: Press mentions, chef credentials, years in business, visible menu depth, delivery-platform logos, high-volume food photography
- Primary keywords repeated across competitors: Vietnamese, pho, menu, authentic or reimagined, reservations, order online, delivery
- Repeated phrases or messaging patterns: Authentic Vietnamese, chef-led or heritage-based story, fresh ingredients, strong broth/flavor claims, menu breadth
- Common buyer objections implied by their messaging: Is the food actually authentic, is this worth the price, is ordering easy, is the place convenient, is the experience special enough to choose over alternatives
- Gaps or weaknesses shared across most competitors: Many competitor sites either over-index on upscale storytelling or on dense menu dumping; several make the brand less immediately legible for a first-time casual local diner looking for a fast decision
