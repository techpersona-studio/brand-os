What I found

Eight issues. One asset you are not using.

Nothing asks the visitor to act

Critical

No consultation button in the nav or the hero. The phone number is plain text. The homepage opens with four paragraphs and ends on a tagline.

The people most ready to hire you have to go hunting.

Your English pages are invisible in search

Critical

/criminal-law is titled "Lawyertruong". Your biography page is titled "Chronicle". No title says Boston, attorney, or a practice area.

Search "Vietnamese criminal lawyer Boston" and your result describes nothing.

The bilingual site is not set up to rank

Critical

You already built both languages, which is the expensive part. But Vietnamese pages need their own URLs, hreflang tags and Vietnamese keywords to surface in search. That layer looks missing.

You paid for the asset. You are not collecting on it.

Your press is a sentence, not a credential

Moderate

The Globe and WCVB Chronicle appear as a text link in paragraph four. No logos, no quote, nothing above the fold.

Your best proof is the easiest thing to skim past.

Duplicate pages are splitting your rankings

Moderate

/real-estate-2 and /auto-accident-1 are both live. Old copies were never removed.

Two half-strength pages competing instead of one strong one.

The site answers on two addresses

Moderate

Both lawyertruong.com and www.lawyertruong.com are indexed, with nothing telling Google which one is real.

Twenty-two years of authority, split in half.

The brand does not hold together

Moderate

Navy seal, navy nav, violet headline, pale blue background. Three colour stories in the first screen. The seal is the good one.

Reads assembled, not designed.

Your reviews are thin and scattered

Moderate

Four on Facebook, none on Lawyers.com, one unanswered negative on Avvo. Nothing on the site asks a happy client for one.

Google reviews decide the map results. Weaker firms outrank you.
