What I found

Twelve issues, in three groups.

Search optimization

5 issues
  • Page titles were never set: /criminal-law is titled "Lawyertruong", your biography page "Chronicle".
  • No title anywhere says Boston, attorney, or a practice area.
  • The Vietnamese pages have no hreflang or language URLs, so they do not rank in Vietnamese.
  • Duplicate pages are still live at /real-estate-2 and /auto-accident-1, splitting the ranking.
  • Both lawyertruong.com and www.lawyertruong.com are indexed, with nothing telling Google which is real.

Design

3 issues
  • Three colour stories above the fold: navy seal, navy nav, violet headline, pale blue ground.
  • The homepage runs four paragraphs at one size, with nothing to guide the eye.
  • Nothing is styled to be clicked, so there is no obvious next step on any screen.

Conversion

4 issues
  • The homepage reads like a biography, not a page built to get a call.
  • No testimonials anywhere, though you have strong ones on Yelp and Google.
  • The Globe and Chronicle credits are a text link in paragraph four, not visible proof.
  • The contact form sits on another page and asks for six fields, including a home address.
