What we found

Nine issues, and one asset you are underusing.

Findings come from the homepage as it renders today and from how the site currently appears in Google's index — the same two views a prospective client gets. A full technical audit covering speed, mobile rendering, form delivery, analytics and accessibility is the first deliverable once we begin.

Nothing on the homepage asks the visitor to act

Critical

There is no consultation button in the navigation and none in the hero. The phone number sits in the header as plain text. The opening screen runs four paragraphs of biography and closes on a tagline rather than a next step, so a reader who has just been convinced has nowhere to go but hunt for a number.

Cost: the visitors most ready to hire you are the ones asked to do the most work.

Your English pages are invisible in search

Critical

The title tag is the blue headline a searcher clicks in Google. Right now /criminal-law is titled "Lawyertruong", the attorney biography page is titled "Chronicle", and the auto accident page is titled "Traffic accident". None contain "Boston", "attorney", or a practice area.

Cost: someone searching "Vietnamese criminal lawyer Boston" sees a result that describes nothing and scrolls past it.

The bilingual build is your biggest asset and it is not set up to be found

Critical

You have already done the expensive part — the site is genuinely bilingual, not machine-translated. But for Vietnamese pages to rank for Vietnamese searches they need their own indexable URLs, correct hreflang pairing, and Vietnamese keyword targeting rather than translated English keywords. On the current evidence that layer is missing, which is why the Vietnamese pages do not surface in search the way the English ones do.

Cost: you have paid for the asset and are not collecting the return on it.

Your press credentials are a sentence, not a credential

Moderate

The Boston Globe and WCVB Chronicle recognition appears as an inline text link in the fourth paragraph of the hero. No masthead logos, no pull-quote, no placement where a hesitant visitor sees it before deciding. The same applies to the Suffolk County ADA appointment.

Cost: the most persuasive material the firm owns is the easiest thing on the page to skim past.

Duplicate pages are splitting your rankings

Moderate

Live URLs include /real-estate-2 and /auto-accident-1. Numbered suffixes mean pages were duplicated during a past edit and the originals were never removed. Google then has to choose between two versions of the same page, and splits the ranking signal across both.

Cost: two half-strength pages competing with each other instead of one strong page.

The site answers on two different addresses

Moderate

Both lawyertruong.com and www.lawyertruong.com are indexed. Without a canonical tag or a redirect picking one, search engines treat them as two separate sites carrying the same content.

Cost: authority earned over twenty-two years is divided in half.

The brand does not hold together above the fold

Moderate

The seal is navy and gold, the navigation bar is navy, the hero headline is violet, and the background is pale blue. That is three unrelated colour stories in the first screen, and the violet in particular belongs to none of them. The seal itself — Lady Justice, "Voice of the Voiceless" — is genuinely distinctive and deserves to set the palette.

Cost: the page reads as assembled rather than designed, for a practice whose product is judgment.

Your review footprint is thin and scattered

Moderate

Facebook shows four reviews and no rating. Lawyers.com carries none. A single negative review from 2017 sits unanswered on Avvo. Yelp has a handful of genuinely strong ones. For local legal search, review volume and recency on Google are among the strongest ranking factors there are — and nothing on the site currently asks a satisfied client for one.

Cost: competitors with a fraction of the record outrank the firm in the map results.

Intake stops at 6pm on Friday

Moderate

The only routes in are a phone number and an email address, staffed Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. Arrests, accidents and immigration notices do not observe office hours, and the client in that moment calls down the list until somebody responds.

Cost: the highest-urgency, highest-value matters arrive when nobody is there to catch them.

The asset: you are already bilingual, and almost nobody is

Strength

A full Vietnamese edition, a distinctive seal, professional photography, news and video sections, twenty-two years in practice, prosecutorial and defence experience on both sides of the bar, and mainstream press credentials. Every expensive ingredient is already bought and paid for.

This is a conversion and visibility problem, not a content problem — which is much the cheaper of the two to fix.

Page speed, mobile rendering, form delivery, analytics coverage, hreflang implementation and accessibility require access to the site's code and are confirmed in the full audit at the start of either engagement.
