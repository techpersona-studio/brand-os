# SEO Foundation Brief

**Client:** Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên
**Prepared by:** TechPersona Studio
**Date:** 2026-06-25

---

### 1. Primary SEO goal

The primary goal is to capture Vietnamese diaspora search intent for weight loss coaching and programs — specifically from Vietnamese-speaking women in the USA, Australia, Canada, Germany, and UK who are searching in Vietnamese or using Vietnamese identity markers in their queries. SEO must position the site as the go-to authority for online giảm cân coaching in Vietnamese, with enough bilingual presence to capture English-Vietnamese searchers. Every page must support the consultation booking path — the site's only conversion event.

---

### 2. Homepage target

The homepage should target the broadest service theme: Vietnamese online weight loss coaching / giảm cân online with an implied USA/diaspora audience signal. It should rank for branded searches (Trần Thủy Tiên, Young Group) and category-defining queries ("giảm cân cho người Việt tại Mỹ," "weight loss coaching Vietnamese online," "chương trình giảm cân tiếng Việt"). The homepage should not attempt to rank for specific city or neighborhood terms — the service is nationwide and multinational. Narrowing to a city would misrepresent the offering and dilute the primary intent signal. Location signals belong in content (testimonials with US states) and structured data, not the primary title tag.

### 5. Homepage SEO role

- **Target theme:** Vietnamese online weight loss coaching — category-defining, not a location page
- **Trust signals that must be structurally present:** follower count (226K Facebook); testimonial results (named, numbered, located); money-back guarantee; service area coverage (six countries); founder identity (name and face)
- **Location targeting:** Do not target a single city or region in the title tag or H1. Include USA as the primary market signal in structured data and service area copy. Testimonials referencing US states serve as implicit location relevance without narrowing the homepage to one city.
- **Breadth vs. specificity:** The homepage should be broad enough to capture all entry-level Vietnamese diaspora weight loss queries, with specific enough proof (named testimonials, follower count, guarantee) to convert. Avoid generic wellness copy that loses the Vietnamese-language specificity signal.

---

### 6. Service page rules

- **Number of core service pages at launch:** One. The weight loss program is the core service and earns the primary Program page. If the skincare program is confirmed as a separately priced, distinct offering, it earns its own page.
- **When a service deserves its own page:** When the search intent is clearly distinct (different buyer, different problem, different keyword set) and the page can be filled with substantive non-thin content. Splitting services that share the same buyer and problem dilutes focus.
- **What each service page must include for SEO:** Program name + primary keyword in H1; unique description of what's included (42-meal combo, personalized by body type, ~15 min prep, no hunger); named testimonials specific to the service; guarantee terms stated clearly; FAQ section covering the main service objections; internal links to Results page and Contact/booking
- **Merge vs. split:** Keep meal plan + coaching + food combo on one Program page — these are components of a single offer, not separate services. Do not split into multiple pages.
- **FAQ and proof signals on service pages:** 3–5 FAQ entries answering "will I go hungry," "how does the meal plan work," "what's in the food combo," "what happens if I don't see results"; testimonial chips with weight + timeframe + US state

---

### 7. Location strategy

Location pages are not justified at launch. The business is online-only, serves six countries, and has no physical location that creates geographic search intent. The primary audience searches by language and cultural identity ("Vietnamese"), not by city. US state references in existing testimonials already create implicit geographic relevance for US searches without generating thin doorway pages.

Defer location pages to section 12. If future analytics show significant organic impressions from specific US metros with large Vietnamese diaspora populations (Houston, San Jose, Westminster CA, Dallas TX), that signal would justify a targeted test.

NAP consistency (Name, Phone, Email) should be maintained on the Contact page and matched exactly to any Google Business Profile entry if the business has a registered US address.

### 9. Internal linking priorities

**Conversion flow — homepage → program → contact:**
The homepage links to the Program page via the primary hero CTA and at least one secondary CTA lower in the page. The Program page links to Contact/Consultation as its primary action — this is the single most critical link path on the entire site. No other link competes with it on the Program page.

**Trust reinforcement — results → program:**
The Results/Testimonials page links back to the Program page after each testimonial cluster, converting trust into action without dead-ending visitors on the results page.

**Intent alignment — FAQ → program and contact:**
Each FAQ answer that resolves an objection links to the relevant Program page section (e.g., "see how the meal plan works") or directly to Contact (e.g., "book a free consultation to ask your specific question").

**About/Founder → program:**
The About page transitions from founder authority into the Program page — the credibility section leads naturally to "here is what the program includes."

**Results/testimonials → program and contact:**
Each named result block includes a contextual "learn more about the program" link to the Program page and a secondary "book a consultation" link to Contact.

### 10. Schema recommendations

**Person schema**
- For Trần Thủy Tiên on the About/Founder page
- Why it matters: Founder-brand queries are a real traffic source; Person schema links the founder identity to the business and helps Google surface an accurate knowledge panel
- Page: About/Founder

**FAQPage schema**
- For the FAQ page and FAQ sections on the Program page
- Why it matters: FAQ schema earns rich result space in SERPs for objection-based queries; directly supports the high-trust-burden buyer who needs questions answered before converting
- Pages: FAQ page + Program page FAQ section

**Organization schema**
- For Young Group on the homepage
- Why it matters: Establishes canonical business identity, social profiles (Facebook, YouTube, TikTok), and service area for Google's knowledge graph
- Page: Homepage

**BreadcrumbList schema**
- For all non-homepage pages
- Why it matters: Clean SERP presentation; helps Google understand the site structure across six required pages
- Pages: All pages except homepage

**AggregateRating schema** — deferred
- Do not implement until a third-party review platform is active. Fabricated or self-hosted review counts in structured data create a policy risk. Add once Google Reviews or another verified platform has a real review count.

### 11. Technical SEO must-haves

**Crawlable structure:** Flat hierarchy — homepage to all main pages in one hop. No content more than two levels deep at launch. Navigation must link to all six required pages.

**URL conventions:** Use Vietnamese slugs with Latin romanization for readability (e.g., `/chuong-trinh-giam-can`, `/ket-qua-khach-hang`) or English slugs if the CMS does not support diacritics in URLs. Pick one convention before launch and do not mix.

**Title/meta ownership:** Each page gets a unique title tag and meta description. Title pattern for service pages: "[Primary keyword] — Trần Thủy Tiên / Young Group." No two pages share a title tag.

**hreflang:** Not needed at launch. The site is Vietnamese-language only; the audience is globally distributed but content language is uniform (vi). Add hreflang only if an English-language version is introduced.

**Sitemap and robots:** Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console on launch. robots.txt allows all six main pages; block only admin, checkout, or internal tooling paths.

**Image alt approach:** All founder photography gets descriptive Vietnamese alt text naming the founder and page context ("Trần Thủy Tiên — huấn luyện viên giảm cân online"). Testimonial photos (if any) get named client alt text. No generic alt text.

**Heading hierarchy:** One H1 per page containing the page's primary keyword. H2s for major sections. H3s for FAQ items and testimonial groups. No skipped heading levels.

**Core Web Vitals priorities:**
- LCP: The founder hero image is likely the LCP element. Preload it; serve in WebP or AVIF.
- CLS: Be Vietnam Pro and Playfair Display must use `font-display: swap` with `size-adjust` fallback to prevent layout shift on font load.
- INP: Minimize JavaScript. The consultation form is the primary interactive element and must respond within 200ms.

**NAP consistency:** Name (Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên), US phone ((609) 318-3681), and email must appear consistently on the Contact page and match any Google Business Profile entry exactly.

### 13. Open SEO questions

1. **Client count:** What is the specific number of clients who have completed the program? "Thousands" is vague for structured proof. A number like "500+ clients" is the single most impactful SEO trust signal currently missing.

2. **Language of consultations:** Are consultations conducted entirely in Vietnamese, or bilingual? This determines whether any English-language SEO targeting makes sense, or whether the site is Vietnamese-only across all pages.

3. **US physical address:** Does the business have a registered US address? This determines whether a Google Business Profile can be verified — a significant local trust signal for US diaspora searches.

4. **Skincare program:** Is there a standalone skincare program with distinct pricing, testimonials, and buyer intent that warrants its own page at launch?

5. **CMS / platform:** What platform will the site be built on? This affects URL structure decisions, schema implementation method, and Core Web Vitals baseline.

6. **Video testimonials:** Are there video testimonials on YouTube that can be embedded on the Results page? Video content is a strong engagement signal for high-trust-burden buyers.

7. **Vietnam-market targeting:** Should the site also serve a Vietnamese-domain audience in Vietnam (.vn or geo-targeted)? This determines whether hreflang or geo-targeting configuration is needed.

8. **Third-party review platform:** Is the client willing to use Google Reviews or a Vietnamese-language review platform? This is the most direct path to enabling AggregateRating schema and reducing the current trust burden from skeptical buyers.
