# Vietnamese Voice Guide

**Client:** Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên
**Date:** 2026-06-25

---

## 1. Business context

- **Category:** Online weight loss coaching — health and wellness
- **Service type:** Personalized program (meal plans, food combo, 1:1 consultation path)
- **Positioning:** Mid-to-premium ($699, withheld pre-consultation) — personal authority brand
- **Trust sensitivity:** High — skeptical audience with repeated failure history; no third-party review presence
- **Conversion type:** Consultation booking → enrollment

---

## 2. Tone and communication style

- **Tone:** Warm and direct — coach-to-person, not clinical or institutional
- **Sentence style:** Short, scannable — Vietnamese diaspora audience skims; many read in a second language
- **Emotional tone:** Reassurance-first, results-grounded — "this is possible for you" before "here's the method"

---

## 3. Trust language system

- **Primary trust signals (must appear prominently):**
  - Kết quả thực tế — named client outcomes with specific weight numbers and locations
  - Cam kết hoàn tiền — double money-back guarantee (stated assertively, not hedged)
  - Cộng đồng quy mô lớn — 226,000 người theo dõi (cited with exact figure)

- **Secondary trust signals (supporting):**
  - Cá nhân hóa theo thể trạng — proof that plans are built for the individual
  - Tác giả sách "Béo ơi vĩnh biệt" — book authorship as credibility anchor (confirm publish type before amplifying)
  - Hỗ trợ nhiều quốc gia — six-country reach signals scale and reliability

- **Optional trust signals:**
  - Thời gian nấu chỉ 15 phút — supports the simplicity claim when used next to client results
  - Chương trình đã đồng hành với hàng nghìn khách hàng — cite specific client count once confirmed

- **Phrases to use:**
  - "kết quả thực tế" — for proof claims (not "hiệu quả vượt trội")
  - "lộ trình giảm cân" — for the program (not "chương trình giảm cân")
  - "cá nhân hóa theo thể trạng" — for personalization (not literal "thiết kế riêng theo body type")
  - "không cần nhịn ăn" — for hunger objection (direct counter, conversational register)
  - "cam kết hoàn tiền" — for guarantee (assertive, not softened)
  - "đăng ký tư vấn" / "nhận tư vấn" — for CTA (not aggressive push language)

- **Phrases to avoid:**
  - "hành trình lột xác" — transformation framing is aspirational without proof; conflicts with results-first positioning
  - "đánh thức phiên bản tốt nhất của bạn" — generic wellness cliché; trust system requires concrete anchors
  - "giảm cân đẳng cấp" — premium positioning without grounding reads as hollow in this category
  - "bí quyết độc quyền" — implies secrecy; this brand's trust mechanism is transparency and specificity
  - "cam kết thay đổi cuộc đời" — vague; replace with the specific guarantee language already on site
  - "chương trình giảm cân" — use "lộ trình giảm cân" instead (feels guided and personal)

## 5. CTA system

- **Primary CTA direction:** Consultation booking (soft entry before enrollment)
- **Primary CTA style:** Low-pressure, action-first — invitation to get information, not to buy
- **CTA verbs:** Đăng ký, Nhận, Tìm hiểu
- **CTA tone:** Warm and direct — "bạn" address, no imperative pressure
- **Example CTAs:**
  - "Đăng ký tư vấn miễn phí"
  - "Nhận tư vấn lộ trình phù hợp với bạn"
  - "Tìm hiểu lộ trình giảm cân của bạn"
  - "Nhận tư vấn ngay hôm nay"

---

## 6. Content patterns

- **Headline style:** Problem-solution or result-first — state what the client gets or what problem this solves; never lead with program features
  - Pattern: "[Outcome] — không cần [common obstacle]"
  - Example structure: "Giảm [X kg] trong [timeframe] — không cần nhịn ăn, không cần tập gym"

- **Section style:** Short bullets with anchors — state the benefit, then the proof in the same block; avoid paragraph-only sections for key claims

- **Proof style:** Results-first — name + weight number + location + timeframe; always specific, never aggregate-only
  - Pattern: "[Chị X], [US State] — giảm [Y kg] trong [Z tuần]"
  - Support with Facebook follower count (exact figure) as social volume signal
  - Guarantee language repeats twice — this pattern is already validated; maintain it on new pages

## 7. Market alignment

References not provided — patterns inferred from industry norms and confirmed site copy.

**Patterns to adopt:**
- Lead testimonials with weight number + name + location — specific identity markers build credibility with Vietnamese diaspora audiences who distrust anonymous reviews
- Use the founder's name and face as the primary trust vehicle — personal authority brands in this space outperform institutional framing with this audience
- State the guarantee in two distinct phrasings on the same page — already validated on current site; replicate in new page structures
- Use "bạn" address throughout — second-person direct address is the standard for coaching content in Vietnamese; third-person feels cold

**Patterns to avoid:**
- Avoid stacking aspirational adjectives ("tuyệt vời," "phi thường," "đỉnh cao") without a number or name behind them — Vietnamese readers in this category have high skepticism and filter out unanchored claims
- Avoid using "cao cấp" or "đẳng cấp" as primary positioning — premium framing without result-grounding reads as marketing, not proof
- Avoid long service-description blocks before proof — in this category, results must appear before methodology or the reader disengages
- Avoid generic wellness imagery language ("hành trình khỏe mạnh," "cuộc sống cân bằng") — this brand's competitive edge is specificity; lifestyle-editorial tone dilutes it
