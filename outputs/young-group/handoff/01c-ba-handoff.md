## 4. Brand Personality & Voice

**Core traits:**
- Direct and specific — uses exact numbers (pounds, pounds per day) rather than vague claims
- Warm and culturally grounded — Vietnamese language, female honorifics, diaspora-aware framing
- Confident in results — guarantee language is assertive, not hedged
- Simple-first — consistently frames the program as easy, not effortful
- Empathetic to failure history — testimonials front the "tried everything" narrative
— Recommended (strong). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (hero copy, testimonials, guarantee section, program description)

**Anti-traits:**
- Not vague or adjective-heavy — specificity is the brand's trust mechanism
- Not clinical or medical in tone — despite health outcome testimonials, the brand speaks like a coach, not a doctor
- Not English-first — the audience is Vietnamese-speaking; English framing would break trust
- Not aspirational without proof — every claim on the site is paired with a specific number or a testimonial
- Not effort-forward — the brand never leads with how hard you'll work
— Recommended (strong). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (observed copy patterns across hero, proof, and guarantee sections)

**Voice rules:**
- Lead with the outcome, then the method — never lead with program features
- Use exact numbers whenever possible; round numbers read as invented
- Pair every claim with a name, a number, or a guarantee
- Keep sentences short — this audience is skimming in a second language for many readers
- Use "bạn" (you) framing — direct address, not third-person beneficiary language
— Recommended (strong). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (observed copy structure across sections)

**Messaging patterns:**
- "[X pounds] in [timeframe] — [client name], [US state]"
- "Không cần nhịn ăn. Không cần đói." (No starving. No hunger.)
- "Đơn giản - Dễ dàng - Hiệu quả" (Simple - Easy - Effective)
- "Nếu không hiệu quả, hoàn tiền 100%" (If no result, full refund)
- "Hàng nghìn khách hàng tại [markets]" (Thousands of clients across [markets])
— Confirmed (partial). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (direct copy lifted from site)

**Words to avoid:**
- "Weight loss journey" — too generic, too English
- "Transform your body" — aspirational without proof, overused in this category
- "Lifestyle change" — implies long effort, conflicts with the simple/easy positioning
- "Sustainable" — clinical framing that distances from the audience's language
- "Program" used generically — always name what's specific about this program
— Recommended (strong). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (category signal analysis; none of these phrases appear in current site copy)

**Brand archetype (optional):** The Caregiver with Sage elements — the brand combines empathetic coaching (caregiver) with confident knowledge transfer (sage). The founder's name and face are central to the brand; it is a personal authority brand, not an institutional one.
— Recommended (strong). Source: https://www.tranthuytien.net/ (founder-named brand, personal testimony positioning, coaching language throughout)
