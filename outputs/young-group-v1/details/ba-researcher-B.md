## Conflicts found

- The Facebook page title emphasizes "Giảm Cân Cho Người Việt Tại Mỹ," while the website uses both U.S. and Vietnam contact addresses. This is not a hard conflict, but it suggests cross-border positioning that should be stated clearly.
- The website identifies the program as a weight-loss system, but no independently verified third-party credentials, licenses, or review counts were found.
- The official site shows a U.S. address in Texas and a Vietnam address in Ho Chi Minh City; whether both are active business locations is not independently verified.

---

## Reasoned recommendations

- Clarify the primary market on the website and social profiles, especially whether the business is mainly for Vietnamese customers in the U.S., customers in Vietnam, or both.
- Add verifiable trust signals such as business registration details, practitioner credentials, and a consistent contact structure across all profiles.
- Publish a clearer service map with program scope, intake process, expected timeline, and what the customer receives.
- If a Google Business Profile exists, keep the name, address, and phone number perfectly aligned with the website and social pages.
- Add independently verifiable reviews or case studies, especially for the target diaspora audience.
