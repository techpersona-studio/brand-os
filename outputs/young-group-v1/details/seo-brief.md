# SEO Foundation Brief

**Client:** Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên
**Industry:** Weight loss coaching / health and wellness
**Primary language:** Vietnamese (international diaspora)
**Date:** June 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

SEO for this business has one job: connect Vietnamese-speaking women across the USA, Australia, Canada, and Vietnam to a 1-on-1 weight loss program they are already searching for but haven't found yet. The search behavior that matters most is intent-driven — women who have tried multiple programs and are now searching for something different, more personalized, and permanent. SEO should funnel that skeptical, trust-hungry audience into the consultation booking flow — not try to rank broadly for "weight loss" in English against global competition the brand cannot beat.

---

## 2. SEO summary

**Primary search opportunity:**
Vietnamese-language weight loss queries across four target markets (USA, Vietnam, Australia, Canada). This is a high-intent, lower-competition niche compared to English-language weight loss. A well-structured Vietnamese site with genuine proof content can rank meaningfully with limited SEO overhead.

**Secondary search opportunity:**
English-language searches by Vietnamese diaspora and bilingual Vietnamese-Americans who search in English but respond to Vietnamese cultural framing — queries like "weight loss coach Vietnamese women" or "personalized weight loss program no exercise."

**Biggest SEO advantage:**
Brand differentiation maps directly onto high-intent search phrases. "Giảm cân không cần tập thể dục," "giảm cân không cần kiêng," "giảm cân cho đến khi đạt mục tiêu" — these are specific enough to own with minimal competition. The four pillars (no supplements, no exercise, no extreme dieting, no regain) are ready-made search themes.

**Biggest SEO risk:**
No confirmed credentials means authority in the health/wellness category is thin. Google's E-E-A-T guidelines treat health content with elevated scrutiny. Without credentials, the site must compensate through named proof, the founder's published book, specific outcome data, and a transparent process — not vague wellness positioning.

**What the site must be structured to capture:**
Service intent queries (what the program is), trust/comparison queries (is this legit, does it work, vs other programs), problem-aware queries (I've tried everything, why can't I lose weight), and branded queries as reputation grows.

## 3. Search intent map

**Service intent**
- Searcher wants: a personalized weight loss program that runs 1-on-1
- Conversion intent: high
- Best page type: homepage + primary service page

**Problem/symptom intent**
- Searcher wants: to understand why previous programs failed and what would actually work
- Conversion intent: medium-high (research stage, converts when trust is built)
- Best page type: narrative sections on homepage, FAQ page, "Tại sao" (Why it works) content

**Trust/comparison intent**
- Searcher wants: proof this is real — reviews, results, who the coach is, what the guarantee covers
- Conversion intent: high (pre-close research)
- Best page type: results/testimonials page, about/founder page, FAQ page

**Community/location intent**
- Searcher wants: a program for Vietnamese women specifically, in their country/context
- Conversion intent: medium-high
- Best page type: homepage (already positioned for Vietnamese diaspora), optional country landing pages

**Branded intent**
- Searcher wants: to find Trần Thủy Tiên directly after a referral, Facebook post, or word of mouth
- Conversion intent: very high
- Best page type: homepage — must rank #1 for "Trần Thủy Tiên" and "Young Group giảm cân"

**Commercial investigation intent**
- Searcher wants: to compare this program against AIH, other Vietnamese weight loss programs, or generic coaching programs
- Conversion intent: high
- Best page type: FAQ page (comparison/objection-handling questions), about/founder page, process page

**Educational intent**
- Searcher wants: to understand root-cause weight loss, why diets fail, what personalized coaching involves
- Conversion intent: low-medium (top of funnel)
- Best page type: blog (later phase), FAQ page (now), narrative sections on homepage

**Emergency/urgent intent**
- Not applicable. Weight loss is a chronic pain point, not an emergency category. Do not design for urgency signals that contradict the brand.

## 5. Page strategy

### Homepage
- **Purpose:** Establish brand authority, communicate the primary offer, capture branded and service intent traffic, and drive consultation bookings.
- **Primary intent:** Service intent + branded intent
- **Must contain:** Founder presence above the fold, core promise (runs until goal reached), four pillars, proof/testimonials, guarantee statement, process overview, consultation CTA (minimum 3 instances)
- **Required now:** Yes

### Primary service page — Chương trình giảm cân 1-1
- **Purpose:** Dedicated page for the core program. Captures deeper service intent searches. Lets the homepage stay conversion-focused rather than explanation-heavy.
- **Primary intent:** Service intent + commercial investigation
- **Must contain:** Full program description, what's included (meal plan, food combo, daily tracking, Zoom sessions, lifetime formula), process steps in detail, FAQ block, guarantee, consultation CTA
- **Required now:** Yes — this is the most important page after the homepage

### About / Founder page — Trần Thủy Tiên
- **Purpose:** Capture branded queries, build trust with skeptical buyers, establish the founder's authority.
- **Primary intent:** Trust/branded intent
- **Must contain:** Founder story and methodology, personal connection to the weight loss journey, book mention ("Béo ơi vĩnh biệt"), client outcome statistics (when confirmed), credentials (when confirmed), consultation CTA
- **Required now:** Yes

### Results / Testimonials page
- **Purpose:** Dedicated proof hub. Captures "review" and "does it work" queries. Aggregates all named testimonials and outcome data.
- **Primary intent:** Trust intent
- **Must contain:** All named testimonials (name, location, result, timeframe, quote), results chips with aggregate numbers, before/after context framing (narrative, not gym-culture), guarantee restatement
- **Required now:** Yes — trust burden is too high to leave proof buried on the homepage

### FAQ page
- **Purpose:** Catch problem-aware, comparison, process, and pricing queries. Answer the objections that prevent consultation bookings.
- **Primary intent:** Problem/symptom intent + commercial investigation
- **Must contain:** Why previous programs fail (root-cause framing), how this program is different, pricing/consultation model, shipping details for food combo, who the program is for, guarantee terms, credentials context
- **Required now:** Yes

### Contact / Book consultation page
- **Purpose:** Capture consultation intent. The conversion endpoint.
- **Primary intent:** Service intent (action)
- **Must contain:** Booking form (name, contact, country, goal), clear confirmation, contact details (Zalo, email, phone), expected timeline for response
- **Required now:** Yes

### Blog / insights section
- **Purpose:** Long-tail educational content, community-building, root-cause content marketing.
- **Required now:** No — launch without. Add in Phase 2 when core pages are ranking and proof is established. Blog content too early dilutes focus without SEO payoff.

### Country landing pages (USA, Vietnam, Australia, Canada)
- **Purpose:** Capture community/location intent per market. Differentiate content by country-specific context.
- **Required now:** No — later. Add only if organic traffic data shows meaningful volume by country that the homepage isn't capturing. Start with homepage targeting all four markets.

## 6. Homepage SEO role

**Homepage target theme:** Vietnamese 1-on-1 weight loss coaching — personalized, no extreme methods, runs until goal reached.

**What trust signals must appear:**
- Founder name and face (above the fold — primary trust anchor)
- Guarantee statement (money-back if no results)
- Named testimonials with specific results
- Four differentiating pillars (no supplements, no exercise, no extreme dieting, no regain)
- Client count (when confirmed) — "Đã đồng hành cùng [X] người"
- Book mention (secondary trust signal)

**Location targeting:**
The homepage should reference the multi-market audience (USA, Việt Nam, Úc, Canada) without trying to rank for individual city terms. Do not over-optimize for a single location. The service area copy ("Phục vụ phụ nữ Việt tại Mỹ, Úc, Việt Nam, Canada") is sufficient for geographic relevance signals.

**Broad vs. specific:**
The homepage should rank for branded terms and the primary program theme ("giảm cân cá nhân hóa," "giảm cân 1-1"). It should not try to rank for broad terms like "giảm cân" or English terms like "weight loss program" — that competition is unwinnable at this domain authority level.

**CTA model the homepage supports:**
Single, persistent CTA: book a free consultation. Every section should support this without duplicating the button in the same viewport.

## 7. Service page strategy

**How many core service pages are needed:** One primary service page for the flagship 1-on-1 program. Possibly one secondary page for the 42-meal food combo as a standalone product page (if it's sold separately — confirm with client). No others needed at launch.

**When a service deserves its own page:**
Only when the service targets a distinct search intent that the homepage cannot satisfy — or when the service has enough differentiating content to justify its own URL and title tag. At launch, one primary service page covers the full program.

**How deep to go:**
The service page should go deeper than the homepage — full process explanation, what's in the meal plan, how daily tracking works, how Zoom sessions are structured, what the lifetime formula contains. This is where commercial investigation intent converts.

**Proof and FAQ each service page should include:**
- 2–3 testimonials specific to the program experience (not just results, but the "what it was like to work with her" dimension)
- FAQ block: 5–8 questions covering pricing model, time commitment, shipping, how personalization works, what happens if you miss a check-in
- Guarantee restated in context: "If you follow the program and see no results, you get a full refund."

**Merge vs. split:**
Keep services on one page at launch. If the client adds distinct service lines (e.g., group programs, corporate wellness, a separate book coaching program), split then.

## 8. Location strategy

**Assessment:** This business is online-first, globally distributed. It is not a local service business. Location pages as traditionally defined (city + service pages) are not the right strategy here.

**What to do instead:**
- Use country-level copy on the homepage to signal relevance ("Phục vụ phụ nữ Việt tại Mỹ, Úc, Việt Nam, Canada")
- Include the USA address and Vietnam address in the footer for NAP consistency and local trust signals
- Maintain a Google Business Profile for the USA address (Midlothian, TX) — this helps local Vietnamese-Americans find the business through Google Maps
- Later: if traffic data shows strong volume from specific cities (e.g., Little Saigon, San Jose, Houston), create lightweight community landing pages — but only with genuinely different content, not thin location duplicates

**What not to do:**
Do not create city pages without unique content. Thin pages targeting "giảm cân tại Houston" or "giảm cân tại Melbourne" with minimal unique content are doorway page territory and create a spam risk.

## 9. Trust / comparison / proof SEO opportunities

**"Does this program work?" / "Có hiệu quả không?"**
- What people confirm: that other customers got real, lasting results
- Which page: Results/testimonials page (primary), homepage proof band (secondary)
- Conversion importance: critical — this is the highest-stakes trust question

**"Is this safe? Do I need to consult a doctor?"**
- What people confirm: that the no-supplement, no-extreme-diet approach is medically sound
- Which page: FAQ page (process + safety questions), service page
- Conversion importance: high — health category requires this

**"Who is Trần Thủy Tiên? Is she qualified?"**
- What people confirm: founder credibility — training, book, client history
- Which page: About/founder page (primary), homepage founder presence (secondary)
- Conversion importance: high — private pricing requires strong founder trust

**"What does the money-back guarantee actually cover?"**
- What people confirm: exact terms, what counts as "no results," how to claim
- Which page: FAQ page (dedicated question), service page
- Conversion importance: high — guarantee is the #1 objection-reducer

**"How is this different from [AIH / other Vietnamese programs]?"**
- What people confirm: that this is not a supplement program, not a clinic, not a group program
- Which page: FAQ page ("how is this different from other programs"), service page (differentiator section)
- Conversion importance: high — Vietnamese weight loss market has competitors, buyers compare

**"What's the price?"**
- What people confirm: that there's a free consultation before pricing is revealed, and that the investment is explained in context
- Which page: FAQ page ("How does pricing work?"), service page
- Conversion importance: medium — price opacity is intentional; FAQ should explain the model, not reveal price

**"Can the food combo ship to [my country]?"**
- What people confirm: shipping destinations, timeline, what's included
- Which page: Service page (product section), FAQ page
- Conversion importance: medium — removes a logistics barrier to booking

## 12. Schema opportunities

**LocalBusiness (priority: high)**
- Why: The business has a confirmed USA address (Midlothian, TX) and a Vietnam address. LocalBusiness schema with `@type: HealthAndBeautyBusiness` signals geographic presence and category relevance.
- Where: Homepage `<head>`, contact page

**ProfessionalService (priority: high)**
- Why: This is a personal coaching service. ProfessionalService schema supports the founder's individual authority and the service-based business model.
- Where: Homepage, about/founder page

**FAQPage (priority: high)**
- Why: FAQ schema generates rich results in Vietnamese-language SERPs. With high trust burden and many pre-conversion questions, these rich results capture commercial investigation traffic directly on the SERP.
- Where: FAQ page (all questions), service page FAQ block

**Review / AggregateRating (priority: medium — conditional)**
- Why: If testimonials are structured as reviews with ratings, review schema can generate star ratings in SERPs — a significant trust signal. This requires actual ratings (e.g., a 5-star scale) per review.
- Where: Results/testimonials page, service page
- Caveat: Only implement if testimonials include explicit ratings. Do not fabricate or infer ratings.

**Person (priority: medium)**
- Why: Trần Thủy Tiên is the brand. Person schema on the about page links the founder identity to the business, supporting E-E-A-T signals for health content.
- Where: About/founder page

**Book (priority: low)**
- Why: "Béo ơi vĩnh biệt" is a published book and a trust signal. Book schema on the about page or a dedicated book mention section signals real-world authority.
- Where: About/founder page (when book details are confirmed and linked)

**BreadcrumbList (priority: medium)**
- Why: Improves SERP display for inner pages. Useful once the site has more than 5 pages.
- Where: All inner pages (service, about, results, FAQ)

## 13. Technical SEO foundation

**Crawlable structure:**
No JavaScript-rendered content for primary SEO pages. The homepage, service page, about page, results page, and FAQ page must be fully server-rendered or statically generated. Client-side rendering of above-the-fold content is a crawl risk for a low-authority domain.

**Logical URLs (use English/transliterated slugs):**
- `/` — homepage
- `/chuong-trinh-giam-can` — primary service page
- `/tran-thuy-tien` — about/founder page
- `/ket-qua` — results/testimonials page
- `/cau-hoi-thuong-gap` — FAQ page
- `/tu-van` — consultation/contact page

Avoid Vietnamese-character URLs in the URL bar. Use ASCII slugs. Vietnamese characters in URLs create encoding issues in some browsers and link-sharing contexts.

**Title and meta ownership:**
Each page needs a unique, keyword-relevant title tag and meta description. No template duplication. Title format: `[Primary keyword] — Trần Thủy Tiên | Young Group`.

Example:
- Homepage: `Giảm cân cá nhân hóa từ gốc rễ — Trần Thủy Tiên | Young Group`
- Service page: `Chương trình giảm cân 1-1 cho đến khi đạt mục tiêu | Young Group`
- FAQ: `Câu hỏi thường gặp về chương trình giảm cân | Young Group`

**Canonical rules:**
Set self-referencing canonicals on all pages. If a Vietnamese-language and an English-language version of the same page exist, use `hreflang` (see below) rather than canonicaling one to the other.

**Multilingual (hreflang):**
The site is currently Vietnamese-only. If an English version is added (for Vietnamese-Americans who search in English), implement `hreflang="vi"` / `hreflang="en"` tags. Do not create English versions without genuinely translated content — machine-translated pages damage trust and rankings.

If Vietnamese variants are needed per country (e.g., `vi-US`, `vi-AU`), implement `hreflang` country variants. However, for launch, a single Vietnamese page per topic is sufficient.

**Sitemap:**
Generate an XML sitemap including all indexable pages. Submit to Google Search Console. Exclude the consultation booking confirmation page from the sitemap.

**Robots:**
Block consultation confirmation pages and admin areas. Allow all primary content pages.

**Image alt text:**
- Founder portrait: `alt="Trần Thủy Tiên — chuyên gia giảm cân cá nhân hóa"`
- Client photos: `alt="[Client name] — giảm [X]kg trong [Y] tháng với chương trình Young Group"`
- Food combo: `alt="Bộ 42 bữa ăn giảm cân được tính toán cá nhân hóa — Young Group"`
- Decorative elements: `alt=""`

**Heading hierarchy:**
One H1 per page. The H1 must include the primary keyword theme for that page. H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections and card titles. Do not skip heading levels.

**Core Web Vitals:**
- LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): The founder portrait hero image is the likely LCP element. Optimize: serve as WebP, eager-load, use `srcset` for responsive sizes, preload with `<link rel="preload">`.
- CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): Reserve space for fonts with size-adjust. Reserve image dimensions with width/height attributes.
- INP (Interaction to Next Paint): The consultation booking form and navigation interactions must be responsive. Avoid heavy JavaScript blocking the main thread.

**NAP consistency:**
- USA: 3428 Burning Tree Lane, Midlothian, TX 76065 | (609) 318-3681
- Vietnam: 33 đường 66, phường 10, quận 6, TP HCM
- Email: coachthuytien@gmail.com
- Zalo: 0765 480 529

These must appear consistently in the footer and contact page. Match exactly any existing Google Business Profile or directory listings. Inconsistency between on-site NAP and GBP NAP hurts local trust signals.

## 16. Unknowns / client questions

1. **Credentials:** Do you have any formal certifications, licenses, or qualifications in nutrition, health coaching, or wellness? (Affects: about page content, credential strip activation, E-E-A-T authority — critical for health content rankings.)

2. **Client count:** What is the total number of completed clients? A specific number ("Đã đồng hành cùng hơn [X] người") is a key homepage trust signal and structured data candidate.

3. **Google Business Profile:** Is there an active GBP for the USA address? If yes, what is its current NAP — must match exactly what we publish on-site.

4. **English-language audience:** What percentage of your clients find you in English vs. Vietnamese? This determines whether an English-language variant of key pages is worth building now vs. later.

5. **Book availability:** Is "Béo ơi vĩnh biệt" currently available for purchase and where? If it has an ISBN or publisher page, it can be referenced with Book schema.

6. **Food combo shipping:** What are the confirmed shipping destinations and timelines? This is a conversion barrier for Australia and Canada clients — needs clear, specific copy.

7. **Testimonial approvals:** Which of the 6+ named testimonials on the current site are approved for use on the new site? Are photos available for any of them?

8. **Primary language split per market:** Does your Vietnam audience primarily find you through Facebook, or are they also searching on Google Vietnam? This affects how aggressively to optimize for Vietnamese Google SERPs vs. social referral traffic.

**SERP observations (qualitative — no fabricated data):**
- Vietnamese-language weight loss queries ("giảm cân cá nhân hóa," "chương trình giảm cân 1-1") show lower competitive density than English equivalents. This is a meaningful opportunity for a site with genuine proof content.
- Vietnamese diaspora searches often blend Vietnamese and English keywords. "Weight loss coach Vietnamese" and "giảm cân cho người Việt tại Mỹ" are distinct intent signals — both worth targeting.
- Health/wellness categories in Google SERPs are subject to E-E-A-T scrutiny (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Thin credentials + health claims without proof create ranking risk. The site must lead with real evidence: named outcomes, founder story, published book.
