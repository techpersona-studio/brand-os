# Website Blueprint Handoff
**Client:** Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên
**Sites analyzed:** tranthuytien.net · tranthuytien.store
**Date:** June 2026
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Current site verdict

- **Overall status:** Single-page promotional landing page. Functional but structurally inadequate for the trust burden and purchase complexity of this offer.
- **Core conversion problem:** Fake-urgency mechanics (expired countdown timer, perpetual "50% off" framing) actively undermine the credibility required for a high-ticket, private-pricing health service.
- **Core trust problem:** No dedicated about/founder page, no FAQ, no standalone results page — the full trust-building argument is compressed onto one scroll. Insufficient for cold buyer due diligence.
- **Core style problem:** Visual execution reads as generic Vietnamese page-builder promotional template circa 2020. It does not match the premium, founder-led, warm luxury wellness positioning defined in the Branding Visual Guide. Triggers low-trust category associations (supplements, MLM, diet programs) before content can overcome it.
- **Core structural problem:** One page doing all jobs — hero, proof, offer, about, CTA, contact — with no SEO-indexable page architecture. Zero ability to capture problem-aware, comparison, or branded search traffic outside the homepage URL.
- **Secondary brand conflict:** tranthuytien.store (exercise equipment) directly contradicts the flagship program's "no exercise required" positioning. Must be resolved before or during redesign.

---

## 2. Recommended intervention level

**Full redesign.**

**Why:** The content assets are mostly sound. The architecture is not. A single-page structure with no dedicated about, FAQ, results, or service pages cannot serve the trust burden, the SEO opportunity, or the buyer journey this program requires. Patching the existing page — removing the timer, updating copy — improves the margin, not the outcome. The visual system requires a rebuild to reach premium execution; the page structure requires a rebuild to support multi-page SEO and deep trust-building.

**Scope implication:** Full site build with at minimum 6 pages (homepage, service page, about/founder, results/testimonials, FAQ, contact/consultation). Visual system built from scratch per the locked Branding Visual Guide. Content migration and rewrite, not a blank slate — existing testimonials, offer language, and guarantee copy are valuable inputs.

- **The guarantee language.** "CAM KẾT KHÔNG HIỆU QUẢ SẼ HOÀN TIỀN" and the 100% refund framing are clear and direct. Keep verbatim, elevate placement.
- **The 80/20 framing.** Exercise = 20%, eating = 80% is a clean, memorable positioning device that reframes competitor methods. Keep as a core homepage argument.
- **The four-pillar differentiation.** No supplements, no exercise, no extreme dieting, no regain — confirmed from Facebook brand image and site copy. Keep as a structural trust banner component.
- **Program contents detail.** Weekly menu, 42-meal combo, daily weight monitoring, weekly Zoom sessions, lifetime maintenance formula — all confirmed. This is a strong offer. Keep the complete description, expand on the service page.
- **Founder's book reference.** *Béo ơi vĩnh biệt* is a real credibility signal. Keep and expand on the about page. Activate Book schema when availability is confirmed.
- **CTA label.** "TƯ VẤN CHO TÔI" is already established and tested. Keep this label for the primary consultation CTA across all pages.
- **Multi-market positioning.** USA, Vietnam, Australia, Canada, Germany, UK client base. Keep the international framing — it differentiates from Vietnam-only competitors.
- **Contact information.** Zalo 0765 480 529, Hotline (609) 318-3681, coachthuytien@gmail.com, both physical addresses. Keep all, standardize in footer.

## 4. What to remove

- **Countdown timer.** Expired, perpetually resetting. Destroys trust for discerning buyers. Remove entirely.
- **"50% off" / "giảm ngay trong 2 ngày" framing.** Positions a premium coaching service as a flash sale. If a promotion exists, handle it as a dated, visible offer — never as a manufactured urgency mechanic.
- **Dense promo-page layout structure.** The current page density and section stacking make the site feel promotional and rushed. The redesign should breathe — generous vertical spacing, alternating narrative and proof rhythms, per the visual guide.
- **Off-brand YouTube video links** (lifestyle/relationship content mixed with weight loss). Remove from primary site flow. Founder's video content should be curated for the coaching context only.
- **tranthuytien.store brand overlap.** Exercise equipment sold under the same founder name while the coaching program is positioned on "no exercise required." Retire the .store domain or rebrand it fully outside the Trần Thủy Tiên identity. This is not a design decision — it is a brand conflict that the website redesign cannot solve on its own but must account for.
- **Generic benefit list format.** The 8-benefit icon list (achieve target weight, slimmer physique, better skin, etc.) is present on nearly every Vietnamese health page. Replace with founder-voice narrative sections and named client story content.
- **Stock-photo or generic imagery.** Per visual guide: no stock photos for testimonials, no gym-culture imagery, no clinical aesthetic.

## 7. Conversion implications

- **Homepage must:** Establish founder authority above the fold, communicate the core differentiator (runs until goal reached), present four pillars, show proof, state the guarantee, and drive to the consultation CTA — without promotional urgency mechanics.
- **Trust must appear:** Founder portrait and name above the fold. Named testimonials with results visible within first 2 screens. Guarantee statement in hero subline and CTA band. Four pillars in trust banner near the top.
- **CTA must:** Appear in hero, mid-page, after testimonials, and in footer. Link to a dedicated consultation page that explains the call format and next steps. One CTA variant only (secondary outline variant for low-stakes sections only).
- **Proof must:** Live on a dedicated `/ket-qua` page. Homepage carries a representative set (3–4 best testimonials) with a link to the full page. Each testimonial: name, location, result, timeframe, quote. No stock photos — real client photos or text-only cards.
- **Booking flow must:** End at a simple consultation form (name, contact, country, goal statement) with clear confirmation and response timeline. Zalo, email, and phone as alternative contact methods below the form.
- **Mobile must:** Thumb-accessible CTAs. Testimonials that render full content at mobile width. No dead countdown blocks. Founder portrait loads above fold on mobile without being cropped into irrelevance.

## 8. SEO / architecture implications

- **Homepage target issue:** Currently has no meaningful SEO structure — no optimized title tag, no schema, no internal linking to subpages (no subpages exist). Homepage must target: "Giảm cân cá nhân hóa," "chương trình giảm cân 1-1," branded terms (Trần Thủy Tiên, Young Group).
- **Missing service / FAQ / trust pages:** All five required inner pages are missing. These represent the highest-priority SEO build after the homepage: `/chuong-trinh-giam-can` captures commercial investigation intent; `/tran-thuy-tien` captures branded trust queries; `/ket-qua` captures proof queries; `/cau-hoi-thuong-gap` captures problem-aware and comparison queries; `/tu-van` is the conversion endpoint.
- **Internal linking issue:** No internal linking exists (only one page). Blueprint must implement the full internal linking logic from the SEO Foundation Brief: homepage → service → FAQ → consultation; homepage → results → service → consultation; about/founder → service page.
- **Page architecture issue:** Single-page structure has zero SEO depth. Rebuild must introduce the 5-page minimum with logical URL slugs (ASCII, per SEO brief), unique title tags per page, self-referencing canonicals, and FAQPage schema on the FAQ page and service page FAQ block.
- **Technical concern:** Current site appears to be a Vietnamese page-builder (likely Mota or equivalent). Determine whether to rebuild on a proper CMS or framework that supports server-rendered HTML, schema injection, sitemap generation, and clean URL slugs. Client-side-rendered primary content is a crawl risk on a low-authority domain.
- **Brand conflict SEO risk:** tranthuytien.store is indexed and selling exercise equipment. If it remains live, it sends contradictory signals to Google about what the brand does — especially given the coaching site's "no exercise" positioning. Before the redesign goes live, the .store domain should be redirected, noindexed, or fully rebranded.
