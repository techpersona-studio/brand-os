# Branding Visual Guide — Generator Handoff

## North star

Warm luxury wellness rooted in personal authority — premium, human, and never clinical.

## Direction mix

- Primary direction: Editorial / Human — 65%
- Secondary influence: Enterprise / Stable — 35%

## Locked color system

- **Base:** Light — warm cream / ivory (off-white with warm undertone; not pure white)
- **Neutral:** Warm ivory / cream for backgrounds; deep warm charcoal for text
- **Anchor:** Warm gold / champagne bronze — primary brand color for logo, CTAs, decorative marks, section dividers
- **Secondary:** Deep warm charcoal / near-black for headings and structural text (not pure black)
- **Accent:** Soft blush / dusty rose — feminine-luxury tone, locked. Used for proof section backgrounds, founder quote blocks, secondary card fills.
- **Usage ratio:** Neutrals 60% / Gold anchor 20% / Dark 15% / Accent 5%

## Locked typography system

- **Primary reading font direction:** Clean humanist sans — warm, contemporary, highly readable at all sizes. 75% of typographic presence.
- **Accent / display font direction:** Refined calligraphic script — preserved from existing brand, elevated in quality. Used 1–2 times per section for emotional emphasis phrases only, at display scale. Select a premium-grade script typeface, not a generic web script. 25% of typographic presence.
- **Tone:** Human / Editorial hybrid
- **Hierarchy:** Strong — bold display headings with significant weight contrast over body
- **Heading behavior:** Sentence case, tight tracking, expressive weight
- **Body behavior:** Editorial; generous line height; 60–70 character line length; Regular / 400 weight

## Locked layout rhythm

- **Layout mode:** Flow-based with modular proof sections
- **Rhythm:** Alternating — founder/narrative sections alternate with proof sections
- **Density:** Spacious — generous vertical padding per section
- **Hero structure:** Text + founder portrait
- **Section patterns:** Full-width narrative, proof band, founder block, CTA band, 2-column contrast, process section, quote section

## Locked component language

- **Overall feel:** Soft / Rounded
- **Card language:** Bordered with soft warm shadow; thin warm gold or cream border; slight warm elevation
- **Button language:** Primary — filled gold or charcoal, rounded rectangle, premium weight and spacing; Secondary — outline with warm hover fill
- **Proof modules:** Quote blocks with named attribution and specific results; results chips; founder note; trust banner (four pillars); before/after gallery (approved real assets only); process band
- **Signature component:** Founder quote slab — near-full-bleed section, founder's voice in large editorial or script type, warm cream background, founder's signature below

## Locked imagery direction

- **Photography direction:** Founder-led
- **Subject priority:** People (founder first) → Product (food combo) → Proof (approved client photos) → Object detail
- **Mood:** Warm / Intimate
- **Lighting:** Natural / Soft — warm natural light only
- **Composition:** Medium framing, polished candid
- **Illustration / icon direction:** Outline — thin warm-toned outline icons only; used for four "không" pillars and process steps only

## Locked motion level

- **Level:** Restrained
- **Role:** Reveal / Hover
- **Scroll behavior:** Fade-up — content fades up gently on viewport entry

## Signature move

Script calligraphic phrase used once per major section as emotional anchor, combined with a full founder quote slab component as the brand's highest-trust layout element.

## Do / Don't rules

### Do

- Put Trần Thủy Tiên's face and voice above the fold on every entry point
- State the "until goal is reached" guarantee in the hero headline or subline
- Use specific named testimonials — name, location, kg/pounds, timeframe — every time
- Use warm gold as the primary structural and decorative color
- Use the script accent for emotional emphasis only — maximum twice per section
- Place a consultation CTA in the hero, mid-page proof section, and footer minimum
- Keep body copy line length at 60–70 characters
- Design a placeholder credential strip activatable post-client meeting
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` fully
- Keep all backgrounds in the warm cream/ivory family

### Don't

- Use gym-culture or before/after transformation imagery
- Use cold whites, clinical blues, or sterile visual language anywhere
- Use stock photography for testimonials — real names and photos only, or text-only
- Animate proof content or guarantee statements
- Place competing CTAs in the same viewport
- Use countdown timers styled as dashboard widgets
- Use icon-heavy section headers
- Design more than two button variants for the entire site
- Use pure black on pure white — it breaks warmth throughout
- Design a navigation that buries the consultation CTA more than one click away

## Required output sections

The Branding Visual Guide must include:

- Real palette swatches — hex values for cream base, gold anchor, charcoal text, accent
- Actual font recommendations — humanist sans + calligraphic script, with fallbacks
- Section rhythm — annotated homepage layout with section order and pacing
- Component rules — button states, card styles, founder quote slab spec
- Imagery rules — photography direction with concrete do/don't examples
- Do / don't list
- Mockup directions — at minimum: hero, testimonial section, founder block, CTA band
- Design tokens — color, spacing, type scale, border radius values

## Generator constraints

The generator must not:

- Invent business facts, credentials, or service details not in this brief
- Introduce random accent colors outside the locked warm family
- Use generic SaaS or wellness-app defaults (Inter stacks, blue accents, card grids)
- Contradict the high trust burden requirements — nothing may feel lightweight, playful, or promotional in a cheap sense
- Ignore anti-patterns — especially: no clinical UI, no gym-culture imagery, no stock testimonial photos
- Over-stylize beyond the Editorial / Human direction — avoid cold luxury, over-minimal editorial, or over-refined fashion aesthetics
- Remove or de-emphasize the founder's presence — she is the product's primary trust signal
