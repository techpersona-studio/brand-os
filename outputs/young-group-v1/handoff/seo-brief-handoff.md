# SEO Foundation Brief — Young Group / Trần Thủy Tiên

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** June 2026
**For:** Website Blueprint agent

---

## 1. Primary SEO goal

Young Group's SEO priority is to connect Vietnamese-speaking women across the USA, Australia, Canada, and Vietnam to a 1-on-1 weight loss program they are already searching for. The search behavior that matters most is intent-driven: buyers who have tried other programs and are now looking for something root-cause, personalized, and guaranteed to run until the goal is reached. SEO success is measured by consultation bookings, not traffic volume.

---

## 2. Homepage target

The homepage should rank for the primary program identity in Vietnamese: "giảm cân cá nhân hóa," "chương trình giảm cân 1-1," and branded terms (Trần Thủy Tiên, Young Group giảm cân). It should not compete for broad terms like "giảm cân" or English terms like "weight loss program" — that competition is unwinnable at current domain authority. The homepage should reference all four target markets (Mỹ, Úc, Việt Nam, Canada) in copy for geographic relevance signals but should not target individual city terms — those are deferred.

## 5. Homepage SEO role

- **Target theme:** Vietnamese 1-on-1 personalized weight loss coaching — "giảm cân cá nhân hóa," "chương trình giảm cân 1-1," branded terms
- **Trust signals required for SEO:** founder name and presence, money-back guarantee, named client outcomes with specifics, four differentiating pillars, client count (when confirmed), book mention
- **Location targeting:** Reference all four markets in body copy for geographic relevance — do not target individual city terms on the homepage; that is deferred
- **Breadth:** Rank for primary program theme and branded terms only — do not attempt to compete for broad "giảm cân" or English "weight loss" terms
- **CTA intent supported:** Book a free consultation

## 6. Service page rules

One core service page at launch covers the flagship 1-on-1 program. A second page for the 42-meal food combo may be added only if it is confirmed as a standalone purchasable product.

A service deserves its own page when it targets a distinct search intent the homepage cannot satisfy and has enough unique content for its own title tag, H1, and body copy.

Required SEO content on the service page:
- Full program mechanics in plain language
- Numbered process steps
- Guarantee with specific terms ("if you follow the program and see no results, full refund")
- FAQ block: pricing model, time commitment, shipping, how personalization works, what happens if you miss a session
- 2–3 named testimonials focused on the program experience, not just outcomes

Keep services on one page at launch. Split only if a distinct service line with its own search queries is confirmed.

## 7. Location strategy

This is an online-first, globally distributed service — not a local service business. City or region landing pages are not needed at launch.

**At launch:**
- Reference all four markets in homepage copy for geographic relevance signals
- Include USA and Vietnam addresses in the footer for NAP consistency
- Maintain a Google Business Profile for the USA address (Midlothian, TX)

**Later — only if justified:**
- Country landing pages (USA, Australia, Canada, Vietnam): add only if traffic analytics show meaningful volume by market that the homepage is not capturing; build only with genuinely unique content per country, not thin duplicates
- City-level pages (e.g., Houston, San Jose, Melbourne): justified only with evidence of city-specific search volume and locally unique content — do not pre-build

**FAQPage** (priority: high)
- Why: FAQ schema generates rich results in Vietnamese-language SERPs — high value for a trust-heavy, question-driven buyer journey

- Why: Trần Thủy Tiên is the brand; Person schema links founder identity to the business and supports E-E-A-T for health content

**Review / AggregateRating** (priority: medium, conditional)
- Why: Star ratings in SERPs significantly increase CTR for trust-sensitive categories
- Where: Results page, service page

**Book** (priority: low, conditional)
- Why: "Béo ơi vĩnh biệt" is a published authority signal; supports E-E-A-T for health content
- Where: About/founder page
- Caveat: Activate only when ISBN and publisher details are confirmed

**BreadcrumbList** (priority: medium)
- Why: Improves SERP display for inner pages once the site has 5+ indexed pages
- Where: All inner pages

## 11. Technical SEO must-haves

**Crawlable structure:** All primary pages (homepage, service, about, results, FAQ, contact) must be server-rendered or statically generated. No JavaScript-dependent rendering for above-the-fold SEO content.

**URL conventions:** ASCII slugs — transliterated Vietnamese, no diacritics in the URL path.
- `/chuong-trinh-giam-can` — service page
- `/tran-thuy-tien` — about/founder
- `/ket-qua` — results/testimonials
- `/cau-hoi-thuong-gap` — FAQ
- `/tu-van` — contact/consultation

**Title and meta:** Unique title tag and meta description per page, no duplicates. Format: `[Primary keyword] — Trần Thủy Tiên | Young Group`

**Canonical rules:** Self-referencing canonicals on all pages. If English variants are added later, use `hreflang` rather than canonicaling one language to the other.

**Multilingual (hreflang):** Primary site is Vietnamese. If English variants are added for bilingual Vietnamese-Americans, implement `hreflang="vi"` / `hreflang="en"`. Only build English pages with genuinely translated content — machine translation is not acceptable.

**Sitemap:** XML sitemap covering all indexable pages, submitted to Google Search Console. Exclude booking confirmation page.

**Robots:** Allow all primary content pages. Block admin areas and confirmation pages.

**Image alt text:**
- Founder portrait: descriptive + keyword ("Trần Thủy Tiên — chuyên gia giảm cân cá nhân hóa")
- Client photos: name + outcome context
- Food combo: descriptive product alt
- Decorative elements: `alt=""`

**Heading hierarchy:** One H1 per page containing the primary keyword theme for that page. H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections. Do not skip heading levels.

**Core Web Vitals priorities:**
- LCP: Founder portrait hero image is the likely LCP element — serve as WebP, eager-load, use `srcset`, preload with `<link rel="preload">`
- CLS: Reserve image dimensions with width/height attributes; use `font-display: swap` with `size-adjust`
- INP: Keep the main thread clear of heavy JavaScript, especially on the booking form and navigation

**NAP consistency:** USA address (3428 Burning Tree Lane, Midlothian, TX 76065) and Vietnam address (33 đường 66, phường 10, quận 6, TP HCM) must appear identically in the footer, contact page, and Google Business Profile — character-for-character match.

## 12. Optional later pages

**Blog / insights section**
- What: Long-tail educational content — root-cause weight loss, why diets fail, Vietnamese nutrition context
- When to add: After core pages are indexed and ranking; do not launch before primary service and proof pages are established
- Justification: Consistent organic traffic to FAQ and homepage suggesting an engaged top-of-funnel audience; editorial capacity to produce and maintain it

**Country landing pages (USA, Vietnam, Australia, Canada)**
- What: Country-specific variants with localized content per market
- When to add: Only when traffic analytics show meaningful volume by market that the homepage is not capturing
- Justification: Clear, consistent traffic segmentation by country with enough genuinely different content per market to avoid thin duplication

**City-level pages (Houston, San Jose, Melbourne, etc.)**
- What: Hyperlocal pages targeting Vietnamese community hubs in specific cities
- When to add: Only with evidence of city-specific search volume and unique, useful content per city
- Justification: Confirmed high-volume Vietnamese diaspora queries at city level; local context that differs meaningfully from the country page

**Book page ("Béo ơi vĩnh biệt")**
- What: Dedicated page for the founder's book with purchase link, excerpt, and context
- When to add: When the book has a confirmed purchase destination and the client wants to actively promote it
- Justification: Book availability confirmed; branded + book-title queries showing in Search Console

**English-language service pages**
- What: Translated homepage, service page, and about page for bilingual Vietnamese-Americans who search in English
- When to add: When English-language query share in Search Console justifies the investment
- Justification: Meaningful English-language traffic from Vietnamese diaspora; capacity for high-quality human translation

## 13. Open SEO questions

1. **Credentials:** Does Trần Thủy Tiên hold any formal certifications, licenses, or qualifications in nutrition, health coaching, or wellness? Health content is subject to E-E-A-T scrutiny — credentials directly affect ranking potential for service and FAQ pages.

2. **Google Business Profile:** Is there an active GBP for the USA address? If yes, what is the current NAP — it must match exactly what appears on-site.

3. **Client count:** What is the total number of completed clients? A confirmed number is needed for homepage copy and potential structured data.

4. **Book availability:** Is "Béo ơi vĩnh biệt" currently available for purchase? ISBN and publisher details are needed for Book schema and E-E-A-T signals.

5. **Primary search channel per market:** Do Vietnam-based clients find the business primarily through Facebook or Google? This affects how aggressively to optimize for Vietnamese Google SERPs vs. treating organic as secondary to social referral.

6. **Food combo shipping:** What are the confirmed shipping destinations and timelines? Inaccurate shipping information in FAQ or service page copy damages trust and creates customer service problems.

7. **Testimonial approvals:** Which named testimonials from the current site are approved for the new site? Are client photos available for any of them?

8. **English-language query share:** Does the current site receive meaningful English-language organic traffic? Search Console data would clarify whether English variants are worth building at launch or later.
